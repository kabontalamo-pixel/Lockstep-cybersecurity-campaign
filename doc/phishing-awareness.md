# Phishing Awareness

Phishing is an attempt to trick someone into revealing sensitive information,
installing harmful software ,approving a login, or sending money. It can arrive
through email ,text messages ,social media, phone calls or fake websites.

## Warning Signs

- An unexpected message asks you to act urgently or claims that your account
  will be closed.
- The sender’s address, reply address or website address looks unusual.
- The message contains spelling, grammar or formatting problems.
- You are asked for a password, PIN, one-time code, recovery code or payment
  details.
- A link leads to a login page that does not match the official website address.
- You receive an MFA prompt or verification code that you did not request.
- An attachment is unexpected, especially if it is compressed, executable or
  asks you to enable content.

## What To Do

- Stop before clicking links, opening attachments or replying.
- Check the sender and web address carefully.
- Go to the official website by typing its address yourself or using a trusted
  bookmark.
- Never share one-time codes, passwords or recovery codes.
- Do not approve an unexpected MFA request.
- Report suspicious messages using your email provider’s report function or your
  university IT helpdesk.
- If you entered details on a suspicious page, change the affected password
  immediately from a trusted device and enable MFA.
- If money may be involved, contact your bank or mobile money provider through
  its official app or verified website.

## Remember

A genuine organisation will not normally ask you to share a password or
verification code. When in doubt, contact the organisation directly using
contact details from its official website.
