# Cybersecurity Checklist

Use this checklist to review the security of your accounts and devices.  
Go through it after getting a new device, changing an important password, or
receiving a suspicious message.

## Accounts

- [ ] Use a long, unique password or passphrase for every important account.
- [ ] Use a password manager to create and store passwords safely.
- [ ] Turn on MFA for your email, student portal, banking, cloud storage and
      social media accounts.
- [ ] Choose the strongest MFA option available, such as a passkey, security
      key or authenticator app.
- [ ] Check recent sign-in activity and remove sessions or devices you no
      longer use.
- [ ] Update your recovery email address and phone number.
- [ ] Avoid sharing personal details publicly that could help someone guess
      your security answers.

## Devices

- [ ] Install updates for your operating system, browser and apps as soon as
      they are available.
- [ ] Turn on automatic updates where possible.
- [ ] Use a passcode, PIN, fingerprint or face unlock to protect your device.
- [ ] Lock your device whenever you leave it unattended.
- [ ] Download apps only from official app stores or trusted websites.
- [ ] Delete apps and browser extensions you no longer use.
- [ ] Back up important files to a secure location.
- [ ] Avoid using public Wi-Fi for banking, shopping or signing into important
      accounts; use mobile data instead where possible.

## Identity Theft

- [ ] Check your bank and mobile-money statements regularly for transactions
      you do not recognise.
- [ ] Turn on transaction alerts where your bank or mobile-money provider
      offers them.
- [ ] Avoid sharing your ID number, date of birth or home address unless it is
      necessary.
- [ ] Keep your mobile-money PIN private and never share it, even with someone
      claiming to be from customer support.
- [ ] Report unfamiliar transactions or suspected identity theft to your bank,
      mobile-money provider or the relevant authority as soon as possible.

## Phishing and Incidents

- [ ] Check the sender’s address and website address before signing in.
- [ ] Do not click unexpected links or open unexpected attachments.
- [ ] Never share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes.
- [ ] Do not approve an MFA prompt you did not request.
- [ ] Report suspicious messages to the relevant service provider or your
      university IT helpdesk.
- [ ] Use the [incident-report template](../templates/incident-report-template.md)
      to record suspected incidents.
